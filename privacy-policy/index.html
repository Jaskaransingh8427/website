Privacy Policy for Auto Lingual TTS

Auto Lingual TTS is a text-to-speech (TTS) engine for Android. It is designed to be selected as the active voice engine by other applications on your device — including e-book and PDF readers, document and notification readers, and accessibility services such as Google TalkBack, Select to Speak, and other screen readers — and to route the text it receives from those apps to the appropriate on-device voice automatically. This Privacy Policy explains, in full, what information the App accesses, processes, or stores in every one of these contexts, and describes your rights and choices as a user.

01Introduction & Scope

Code Galaxy Software Solutions ("we," "us," "our," or the "Developer") operates the Auto Lingual TTS Android application (the "App"). Auto Lingual TTS functions as an installable text-to-speech engine on the Android platform: it registers itself with the Android operating system so that it can be selected, by you or automatically by another application, as the engine that converts text into spoken audio.

In practice, this means the App is commonly used in one of two ways: (a) directly, when you provide or select text within the App itself; or (b) indirectly, as the configured voice engine for other applications and services — including e-book readers, PDF and document readers, news and article readers, messaging and notification-reading utilities, and accessibility services such as Google TalkBack, Select to Speak, Voice Access, and other third-party screen readers — which send text to the App through Android's standard text-to-speech framework for it to detect the language and synthesize into speech. Sections 4 and 5 of this Policy describe both scenarios in full technical and practical detail.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how the App handles information — including, most importantly, what it does not collect — regardless of which application or service invoked it, when you download, install, or use it. It applies to the App across all versions, updates, and distribution channels, including the Google Play Store and any other channel through which the App is made available.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using Auto Lingual TTS — whether directly or as the selected TTS engine for another app or accessibility service — you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not install or use the App, and uninstall it if already installed.

This Policy is intended to be read together with the App's Terms of Use (if separately published) and any in-app disclosures. In the event of a conflict between this Policy and an in-app disclosure regarding a specific permission or feature, the more specific, contemporaneous in-app disclosure controls for that feature only. This is the final, published version of this Policy and supersedes any prior draft.

04How the App Works — TTS Engine Architecture

To make this Policy fully transparent, this section describes, in plain terms, how Auto Lingual TTS technically operates as an Android text-to-speech engine, and how it interacts with the other applications that use it.

  1. Registration as a system TTS engine. The App registers itself with the Android operating system as an available text-to-speech engine, the same way Google Speech Services or Samsung TTS does. You (or, in some cases, your device's default configuration) select Auto Lingual TTS as the active engine, either from within the App, or from Android's own Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output menu.
  2. Input from any calling application. Once selected, any application on your device may send text to the App for speech synthesis through Android's standard TextToSpeech API. This includes text you type or select directly within the App, and text sent by third-party host applications — e-book and PDF readers, news readers, messaging apps, or accessibility services — as described further in Section 5.
  3. Local language detection. The App analyzes the received text on-device to determine its language, using logic that runs entirely within the App's own process on your phone. No portion of this analysis is transmitted anywhere.
  4. Local routing. Based on the detected language and your configured preferences (Auto or Mixed mode), the App selects and invokes an underlying text-to-speech voice engine already installed on your device (for example, Google Speech Services or Samsung TTS) through the Android operating system's standard text-to-speech API.
  5. Playback back to the calling application. The selected underlying engine synthesizes audio, which is returned through the standard Android TTS framework and played back through your device's speaker or connected audio output — to the same application that originally requested it. The App does not intercept, record, log, or retain a copy of this text or audio at any stage of this pipeline.

At no point in this flow does text or audio leave your device through any channel operated or controlled by us — regardless of whether the request originated inside the App or from a third-party application using the App as its TTS engine. Whether the underlying TTS voice engine you have selected transmits data off-device is governed entirely by that engine's own privacy policy — see Section 8.

05Accessibility Services, Screen Readers & Google TalkBack

Auto Lingual TTS is built and tested to work reliably as the selected voice engine for accessibility services and assistive technologies, including Google TalkBack, Select to Speak, Voice Access, and comparable third-party screen readers, as well as for e-book readers, PDF viewers, and similar reading applications. Because this is a primary, intended use case for the App, we want to be especially explicit about how it affects your privacy.

5.1 What Changes When You Use an Accessibility Service or Reading App

When TalkBack, another screen reader, or a reading application is configured to use Auto Lingual TTS, the text supplied to the App may include a much broader range of content than text you have chosen to type or select yourself — potentially including, depending entirely on how the host service or application is configured, screen contents, app and button labels, notification text, book or document contents, and other text that the accessibility service or reading app has chosen to read aloud.

5.2 Our Processing Remains Identical, Regardless of Sensitivity or Source

We do not treat text arriving from an accessibility service or a host reading application any differently from text you type directly into the App. The exact same on-device-only pipeline described in Section 4 applies: the text is used transiently, on your device, solely to detect its language and forward it to a voice engine for synthesis. We do not log it, cache it beyond the immediate operation, profile you based on it, transmit it to our servers, or share it with any third party — regardless of how sensitive that text might be.

5.3 What Is Outside Our Control

Because Auto Lingual TTS operates as a system-level engine that any application can call, we have no visibility into, and no control over:

  • Which specific text a host application or accessibility service chooses to send to the App for synthesis;
  • Whether that host application or accessibility service itself collects, logs, or transmits the underlying content elsewhere, independently of the App;
  • Any permissions, screen-reading capability, or data access that Android or the device manufacturer has separately granted to the accessibility service itself (for example, TalkBack's own accessibility-service permissions, which are governed by Google's own privacy policy, not this one).

Responsibility for what content is read aloud, and for that host application's or accessibility service's own data practices, rests with that application or service and with your own device configuration — not with Auto Lingual TTS.

5.4 A Note on Downstream Voice Engines

If you (or your device's default configuration) have paired Auto Lingual TTS with an underlying voice engine that uses network/cloud-based voices rather than fully on-device voices (see Section 8), text read aloud through an accessibility service — which can include highly sensitive on-screen content — could be transmitted by that underlying engine according to its own, separate privacy policy. We encourage users who rely on screen readers, in particular, to review the privacy policy of their selected underlying voice engine and to prefer on-device voice packs where privacy is a priority.

5.5 Our Commitment to Accessibility

We are committed to keeping Auto Lingual TTS reliable and well-behaved for assistive-technology users, and we welcome feedback from users of TalkBack and other screen readers on both accessibility and privacy. You may reach us using the contact details in Section 20.

06Local Storage & Settings

To provide a consistent experience between sessions, the App saves certain preferences locally on your device using Android's DataStore storage mechanism. This includes:

  • Language configurations and language-to-voice mappings you have set
  • Your selected TTS engine(s) for each language
  • Your chosen operational mode (Auto or Mixed)
  • Other in-app preferences, such as speech rate, pitch, or interface settings, where applicable

This information is stored exclusively in the App's private application storage on your device, in accordance with Android's application sandboxing model, and is not accessible to other apps, to us, or to any third party under normal operation.

If you choose to export your settings (for example, to back them up or transfer them to another device), the exported file is encrypted on your device before it is written to storage or shared. We do not receive, store, or have any access to this exported file or its contents at any point. Safekeeping of any exported backup file — including where you choose to store or share it — is your responsibility.

6.2 Uninstallation

Uninstalling the App removes the App's private storage, including all locally saved settings, from your device, subject to standard Android OS behavior (for example, certain devices may retain app data temporarily if "keep app data" options are selected during uninstallation — this behavior is controlled by your device's operating system, not by us).

07Permissions Requested

To function reliably as a low-latency, always-ready text-to-speech engine — including when relied upon continuously by accessibility services and screen readers, where dropped or delayed speech can materially affect a user's ability to operate their device — the App requests the following Android device permissions. We request only what is necessary for the App's core functionality, consistent with Google Play's permissions policies.

PermissionWhy the App requests it
Wake Lock Prevents the device's processor from sleeping mid-operation, so that speech playback is not unexpectedly cut off while the App is actively converting text to speech — including during extended reading sessions initiated by e-book readers or screen readers.
Ignore Battery Optimizations Requests exemption from aggressive battery-management restrictions that could otherwise suspend the App's background service, ensuring reliable, uninterrupted speech feedback when needed, including for users who depend on the App as their primary screen-reading voice engine.
Receive Boot Completed Allows the App to reinitialize its background TTS-engine service automatically after your device restarts, so that TalkBack, other accessibility services, and reading applications can continue to use the App immediately, without requiring you to manually reopen it first.

None of the permissions listed above are used to collect, transmit, or share personal data. Each permission is used solely to support the reliability of the App's on-device text-to-speech engine function, and for no other purpose, including advertising, analytics, or profiling.

You may review, and revoke, any of these permissions at any time — see Section 13 for instructions. Revoking a permission may reduce the reliability of certain features (for example, speech may pause more often, or the App may not restart automatically after a reboot, which may be especially noticeable if you rely on the App together with an accessibility service) but will not disable the App entirely, and will not affect any data privacy protections described in this Policy.

08Third-Party Services & TTS Engines

The App is designed to work with underlying text-to-speech voice engines that are separately installed on your device by you, by your device manufacturer, or by the Android operating system — for example, Google Speech Services or Samsung TTS. Whether this occurs, and how any such data is handled, is determined entirely by the third-party engine you have selected — not by us.
  • We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policy of any TTS voice engine you configure within the App, particularly if you intend to use it — directly or via an accessibility service — to read sensitive or confidential text.
  • Similarly, where the App is used together with a host application (an e-book reader, a screen reader, or another accessibility service), that host application's own privacy policy governs its own separate handling of your data, independent of Auto Lingual TTS.
  • We are not responsible for, and expressly disclaim liability for, the data-handling practices of any third-party TTS engine, accessibility service, host application, operating system component, or other software installed on your device that the App may interact with.

10Data Security

Although the App's architecture is designed to minimize data collection at the source, we take the security of the limited information involved seriously:

  • On-device processing means text and voice data — whatever its source, including from accessibility services and reading applications — never traverse a network connection controlled by us, eliminating an entire category of transmission-related risk for that data.
  • Local settings storage relies on Android's application sandboxing, which isolates the App's private data from other apps on your device under normal operating conditions.
  • Exported backups are encrypted on-device before being written to a file, so that a backup file is not readable in plain text if accessed outside the App.
  • Voluntary correspondence (such as a support email you choose to send us) is handled with reasonable administrative safeguards and access limited to personnel who need it to respond to your inquiry.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and while we and, where applicable, your device's operating system take reasonable steps to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Device-level security (such as using a secure lock screen and keeping your operating system updated) remains an important part of protecting locally stored data.

11Data Retention & Deletion

  • Text and voice data (from any source, including accessibility services): Not retained by us at all, because it is never received by us. Any transient in-memory processing occurs locally on your device and is discarded immediately after the text is spoken.
  • Local settings: Retained on your device only, for as long as the App remains installed, or until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, whichever happens first.
  • Exported backup files: Retained only for as long as you choose to keep the encrypted export file; we have no access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.
